📚 About ROV verification dives — how to read this plan

What this plan is

A verification dive sequence. The AI flagged the high-significance contacts; a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) physically inspects each one to confirm what it is before any installation work. The AI proposes the plan — the dive crew and a qualified surveyor make the final call.

How the order is decided

Dives are grouped by frame (survey-line order) so the ROV travels efficiently, and within each dive the highest-risk target goes first. Co-located targets on one frame are combined into a single transit to save bottom time.

What the tooling means

HD video — close visual identification. Multibeam patch — a 3D acoustic map of the object's extent. Scour profiler — measures seabed erosion around the object. Dimensioning — size and how far it stands proud of the seabed. Pipeline/cable tracker — follows the route and checks burial and free spans.

Burial state

Proud — sits up on the seabed (most hazardous to installation). Partially buried — partly in sediment. Buried / flush — level with or under the seabed.

Important

This is verification only — not a clearance certificate. Declaring an area safe for installation is always a qualified surveyor's decision.
